## Rules for replacements (from the build brief)

- Real photography of Nigerian professionals in genuine career-guidance settings. Natural light, real
  offices, true skin tones, no heavy grading, no filters.
- Never AI-generated people, clay avatars, isometric illustrations or "team high-five" stock.
- Hero: portrait 4:5, at least 1300px tall. Beneficiary: square, at least 1100px.
- Get written model releases for client shoots; store them with the originals.
- Practitioner photos (future): alt text format `Profile photo of [Name], [Specialisation]`.
- Testimonial photos (future): only with the beneficiary's written consent.
